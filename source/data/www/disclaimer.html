WHAT THIS IS

LotusABS is a hobby diagnostic and test tool for the EBC430 ABS unit fitted to some Lotus cars. It is not a professional or certified tool. It is not made, approved or supported by Lotus Cars, by the maker of the ABS unit, or by any vehicle or parts manufacturer. Names of vehicles and parts are used only to describe what the tool is for.

THE RISKS

Using this tool can damage any part of the vehicle and can cause serious injury or death. In particular:

  • OTHER CONTROL UNITS, INCLUDING THE AIRBAGS. The diagnostic line this tool connects to is shared with other control units, including the airbag (SRS) system. The firmware only ever addresses the ABS unit, but any device on a shared line can disturb the others: warning lights (including the airbag light) and stored fault codes in other units are possible. An unintended airbag or pretensioner deployment is very unlikely but cannot be completely ruled out, and a deploying airbag can cause serious injury or death.
  • BRAKES. The Valve Control page opens and closes ABS valves and runs the ABS pump. This changes how the brakes work while it is active, can let air into the brake system, can leave the brakes weak or not working, and can damage the pump or valves if they are left on.
  • FAULT CODES. Clearing trouble codes erases information a workshop may need, and does not fix the fault. A cleared code comes back if the fault is still there.
  • ELECTRICAL. Wrong wiring, a short circuit or a faulty board can damage the vehicle's wiring, fuses, control units or battery, or the tool itself. Leaving it connected can flatten the battery.
  • OTHER. Warning lights, a failed inspection, or a voided warranty can result from using it.
RULES FOR USE
  1. Use it only with the vehicle parked on level ground, engine off, parking brake on, wheels chocked, gear selected. Never while driving, never on a public road.
  2. Connect and disconnect the tool only with the ignition OFF.
  3. While the tool is connected, nobody sits in the vehicle, and keep your head and body out of the path of the airbags (steering wheel, dashboard). Reach in from the side.
  4. Do not leave valves or the pump switched on. Close the Valve Control page when you are done.
  5. Disconnect the tool before driving.
  6. After using Valve Control or clearing codes: check that the brake pedal is firm, that the ABS, brake and airbag warning lights go out, then test the brakes at walking pace in a safe place. If anything is not right, do not drive: have the brakes checked and bled by a qualified mechanic.
  7. If an airbag warning light comes on, have the airbag system checked by a qualified mechanic.
  8. If you are not sure what you are doing, do not use the tool. Have the vehicle diagnosed by a qualified workshop.
